Understudy is installed.

Give a Deployment an understudy:

  apiVersion: apps.understudy.sh/v1alpha1
  kind: Understudy
  metadata:
    name: tenant-worker
    namespace: my-namespace
  spec:
    targetRef:
      kind: Deployment
      name: tenant-worker
    hostageMode: always       # always | voluntary-only | off
    minSurgeTimeSeconds: 60   # your realistic signal -> traffic-ready time
    readinessDeadlineSeconds: 600

Alert on the metric understudy_oldest_blocked_eviction_seconds: a blocked
drain must page a human, never wedge a node.

ESCAPE HATCH - if the operator is ever gone while its PDBs still block
drains, every PDB it owns is labeled for one-command removal:

  kubectl delete pdb -A -l understudy.sh/owned=true
